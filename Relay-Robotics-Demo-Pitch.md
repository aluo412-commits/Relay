# Relay — Robotics Team Demo Pitch

## One-line thesis

**Relay turns the conversations of a robotics team into work that stays visible, verifiable, and moving.**

## 60-second pitch

Relay is an AI execution layer for teams that move fast and communicate constantly.

A robotics team can spend hours building, testing, and solving hard problems — but still lose time because an important detail was buried in chat, a task was never updated, or a teammate did not know they were blocked.

Relay changes that. People keep talking naturally. Relay turns those conversations into real tasks, decisions, and shared knowledge. Then it follows the work: it looks for evidence of progress, notices when something is blocked or stale, and asks the right person the right question.

Unlike a traditional project-management tool, Relay does not ask a team to maintain a second system. Unlike a chatbot, it does not stop at answering. It keeps the team’s picture of reality current and helps move the work forward.

**Chat is for people. Work runs on Relay.**

## Demo sequence

### 1. Start with a real field note

> “The intake is finally consistent. Controller wiring is next — autonomous testing can’t start until that lands.”

Show that this is just a normal log entry. No form. No status meeting.

### 2. Show what Relay understands

Relay identifies:

- Intake work is complete
- Controller wiring is the next active task
- Autonomous testing depends on controller wiring
- The dependency matters to another teammate

### 3. Show the follow-through

Relay keeps the task evidence, connects the dependency, and surfaces the blocker to the right person.

The important moment is not that Relay summarized the sentence. The important moment is that the team can now act on it.

### 4. Close on the difference

Traditional tools ask people to keep the system updated.

Relay watches the work people are already doing, builds the shared picture, and follows up when that picture becomes uncertain.

## Closing line

**The robot is hard enough to build. Relay makes sure the team does not lose the work around it.**
